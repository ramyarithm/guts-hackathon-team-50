# DEAD SIGNAL.
You play as a government telephone engineer sent to an underground communications bunker after several critical telephone systems mysteriously stop working. It's supposed to be a routine maintenance job, but shortly after entering, the heavy security doors suddenly lock behind you, trapping you underground. The emergency lights activate, and your supervisor contacts you through the bunker's intercom, explaining that a security malfunction has triggered an automatic lockdown. He reassures you that he's working on opening the doors from the surface and suggests restoring the damaged communication systems in the meantime. With no other way out, you begin investigating the bunker, unaware that your arrival was no accident. As you repair the first telephone, you recover a damaged recording revealing that the lockdown had actually been authorised hours before you arrived. Confused, you question your supervisor, who dismisses it as a routine security procedure. However, as you continue repairing the remaining systems, you uncover increasingly disturbing evidence. The bunker has secretly been used to monitor civilians, and a maintenance report you submitted weeks earlier had flagged unusual activity within the network. You soon realise that your report had unintentionally exposed a classified surveillance operation. While restoring another telephone, you discover a desperate message left by a missing fellow engineer who had previously been sent to investigate the same bunker. The recording warns you that the supervisor cannot be trusted and reveals that the engineer had discovered a way to escape. Before disappearing, your colleague concealed a four-digit security code across the bunker's damaged communication systems, leaving one fragment in each, hoping someone would eventually uncover the truth. As you recover the fragments, your supervisor becomes increasingly agitated, demanding to know what you've discovered. Eventually, you restore the final emergency terminal and uncover undeniable evidence that your supervisor deliberately sabotaged the communications equipment and arranged the lockdown to lure you underground. He intended to destroy the surveillance records and silence you before you could expose the operation. Realising you've discovered his plan, he cuts off communication entirely, leaving you trapped in the bunker. However, with all four code fragments recovered, you discover an emergency escape route leading through a dangerous underground maintenance tunnel. You make your way through the tunnel, overcoming its hazards until you finally reach a heavily secured exit door. Using the four-digit PIN recovered from the repaired systems, you unlock the door and escape to the surface, carrying the evidence that could expose the entire operation. As daylight breaks and the screen slowly fades to black, one final message appears: "You are out. Now make sure the truth gets out too."
