# Dead Signal Player Dialogue

Recording script for the engineer. Only the player character’s spoken lines are included. Record each numbered cue as a separate clip.

Delivery: conversational and understated at first; increasingly tense as the truth emerges. Pause naturally at ellipses. The tunnel sound cue can be omitted if unused.

## 01  Arriving

> “Underground bunker. Four dead lines. There goes my afternoon.”

## 02  Door locks

> “Hang on… Why’s the door closing?”

## 03  Checking access

> “My card worked thirty seconds ago.”

## 04  After supervisor’s reassurance

> “Fine. You handle the door. I’ll handle the phones.”

## 05  Told to avoid recordings

> “Stored recordings? I’m here to fix a line. Why bring that up?”

## 06  Starting first repair

> “Let’s see what’s keeping you quiet.”

## 07  Discovering the lockdown order

> “Authorised at eight fourteen… I didn’t get here until ten.”

## 08  Questioning supervisor

> “You said it was a malfunction. Why was there a lockdown order before I arrived?”

## 09  After his excuse

> “Right. A precaution.”

## 10  Discovering surveillance

> “Home numbers. Private conversations. None of these people knew.”

## 11  Recognising the fault

> “I know this routing. This is what I flagged in my report.”

## 12  Realising the trap

> “That’s why they sent me.”

## 13  Hearing the missing engineer

> “I remember that name. They said he’d transferred.”

## 14  Learning about the code

> “Four systems. Four digits. He left a way out.”

## 15  Saving evidence

> “I’m copying these recordings. All of them.”

## 16  Supervisor demands answers

> “Just working through the faults. Like you asked.”

## 17  Finding the final log

> “You broke the lines yourself. Then put my name on the callout.”

## 18  Confronting supervisor

> “I’ve got the order. Your voice is on it.”

## 19  Intercom cuts off

> “Hello? …Yeah. Thought so.”

## 20  All fragments recovered

> “That’s the last digit. Time to leave.”

## 21  Entering tunnel

> “He said this leads outside. Please let him be right.”

## 22  Hearing something ahead

> “What was that?”

## 23  Reaching the PIN door

> “There it is. Just one more lock.”

## 24  Wrong PIN

> “No. Slow down. Check the fragments.”

## 25  Door opens

> “Come on… Yes. Yes!”

## 26  Reaching the surface

> “I’m out. Now I need to get these recordings to someone.”
